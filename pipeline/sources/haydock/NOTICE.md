# haydock — Haydock's Catholic Bible Commentary (1859)

*Haydock's Catholic Bible Commentary*, compiled by the Rev. George Leo Haydock
(1774-1849); the 1859 edition, which follows the Douay-Rheims Bible. The
commentary is public domain.

Text shipped: the transcription by John Paul Wohlscheid, published at
<https://johnblood.gitlab.io/haydock/> from <https://gitlab.com/JohnBlood/haydock>
(originally haydock1859.tripod.com). Per its Transcriber's Notes, many
abbreviations of the 1859 text were expanded (e.g. "Aug." to "St. Augustine");
we ship that expanded form. The sister "Confraternity Bible" site under
`confraternity/` is not used.

Each note is anchored to the canonical verse the Douay-Rheims verse it
comments on maps to (Vulgate numbering: LXX psalm numbers with the title as
verse 1; Daniel 13-14 and Esther 11-16 moved to the deuterocanon books).
Citations show the Douay numbering where it differs.
