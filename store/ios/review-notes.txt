Notes for App Review

The app is entirely offline: every text ships inside the app, it makes no network requests, and there is no sign-in. Nothing to configure and no test account is needed.

Text-to-speech uses the system voice (AVSpeechSynthesizer); nothing is sent anywhere.

There are no in-app purchases in this build. The Support screen that appears in the direct-download builds (a link to an outside payment page) is compiled out of App Store builds (SR_DISTRIBUTION=appstore).

Sources and licenses: every text is public domain or CC BY / CC BY-SA, listed with its notice under the book picker > "About the texts".
