# World English Bible Catholic Edition (WEBC)

The World English Bible Catholic Edition is in the public domain; see the
WEB notice for the trademark on the name. Its deuterocanonical books derive
from the Revised Version Apocrypha and Brenton's Septuagint, both public
domain. The edition carries no imprimatur.

Text shipped: the USFM edition from eBible.org (`eng-web-c_usfm.zip`),
pinned by checksum in `sources.toml`. Greek Daniel and the Letter of
Jeremiah are renumbered to this app's canonical scheme at ingest
(`pipeline/sevenreadings_pipeline/deuterocanon.py`); the text is unchanged.
