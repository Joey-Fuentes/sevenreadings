# Haydock's Catholic Bible Commentary (1859)

Haydock's Catholic Family Bible and Commentary, compiled by the Rev. George
Leo Haydock (1774-1849); the 1859 edition (Edward Dunigan and Brother, New
York). Public domain: published in 1859 and its author died in 1849.

Text shipped: the transcription by John Paul Wohlscheid, published at
https://johnblood.gitlab.io/haydock/ from https://gitlab.com/JohnBlood/haydock
(pinned by commit in `sources.toml`). Per its Transcriber's Notes, the
abbreviations of the 1859 text are expanded (e.g. "Aug." to "St.
Augustine"), footnote symbols replaced with bracketed numbers, and Greek
transliterated; we ship that form. The sister Confraternity site under
`confraternity/` is not used.

License of the transcription: the repository and site state none (checked
September 2026: no LICENSE file, no license badge, nothing on the About,
Transcriber's Notes, Free E-Books or Contact pages). We treat the
transcription as public domain, for two reasons recorded here so the
decision is not repeated: the underlying work is public domain, and a
faithful reproduction of a public-domain text with mechanical edits
creates no new copyright — in the United States, where the transcriber is
based, under Feist Publications v. Rural Telephone (1991: "originality, not
sweat of the brow, is the touchstone") and Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel
(1999); in the EU and UK under the Infopaq (C-5/08) originality standard.
Other projects redistribute the same transcription on the same footing.
Should the transcriber publish terms, they take precedence; see
`docs/licensing.md`.

Each note is anchored to the canonical verse the Douay-Rheims verse it
comments on maps to; citations show the Douay numbering where it differs.
