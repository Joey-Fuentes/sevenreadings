# Rashi on Tanakh

The commentary of Rabbi Shlomo Yitzchaki (Rashi, 1040-1105); the Hebrew is
public domain.

English text shipped: from Sefaria's database export
(huggingface.co/Sefaria/database_export, pinned by commit in
`sources.toml`). Sefaria records a license for every version it holds; per
book, the pipeline reads the largest English version whose recorded
license is Public Domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, preferring the
"Pentateuch with Rashi's commentary by M. Rosenbaum and A.M. Silbermann,
1929-1934" (Sefaria: digitised by Sefaria, Public Domain) and the "Sefaria
Community Translation" (CC0). Versions Sefaria marks CC-BY-NC or copyrighted
(for example the Metsudah Chumash) are never read. The build prints, per
book, the version and license it used; that list is the record of what
this content contains. At the pinned commit that is: the Rosenbaum-
Silbermann translation (Public Domain) for the Torah; the Soncino Books of
the Bible translations of 1945-1952 (Sefaria records them as CC BY) for
Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, the Twelve, Proverbs, Job, Daniel, Ezra,
Nehemiah and Chronicles; the Metsudah Five Megillot (CC BY) for Song of
Songs, Ruth, Ecclesiastes and Esther; the Sefaria Community Translation
(CC0) for the Former Prophets, Psalms and Lamentations, where it is partial.

Numbering is the Masoretic Text's, mapped to this app's canonical ids at
ingest by following where the Westminster Leningrad Codex's verses landed;
citations show the Hebrew reference where it differs. Where a version is
CC BY or CC BY-SA the license link is
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ or
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/; attribution: Sefaria, and
the version title.
