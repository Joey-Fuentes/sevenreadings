# St. John Chrysostom, Homilies on the New Testament

The homilies of St. John Chrysostom (c. 347-407) on Matthew, John, Acts,
Romans, 1-2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians,
1-2 Thessalonians, 1-2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon and Hebrews, in the English
of the Oxford "Library of Fathers" (1839-1852) as revised for Philip Schaff's
Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, series 1, volumes 10-14 (New York, 1886-1890).
Public domain: published before 1929 and its editors and translators died
more than seventy years ago.

Text shipped: the Christian Classics Ethereal Library's ThML editions
(ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf110 to npnf114), pinned by checksum in
`sources.toml`. Each file declares in its header `<DC.Rights>Public
Domain</DC.Rights>`. CCEL's general copyright page asks that "CCEL works" not
be used commercially without permission; we take from the files only the
public-domain text of the homilies and none of CCEL's own additions (the
staff-written descriptions, the ThML markup, the layout). Faithful
reproduction of a public-domain text carries no new copyright (see
`docs/licensing.md`), and the file's own rights statement is Public Domain;
that is the basis on which the text ships.

Changes: the editors' footnotes are dropped; each homily is anchored to the
passage it names, through to the verse before the next homily. Headings
are the homily numbers of the printed edition.
