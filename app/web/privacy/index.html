Seven Readings

Privacy policy

Seven Readings does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app does with data. Every text in the app is installed with it. The app makes no network connections. Your bookmarks, notes and settings are kept in a database on your own device and are never sent anywhere; uninstalling the app removes them. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party service inside the app.

Reading aloud. The narrator uses your device's own text-to-speech service. That service belongs to your operating system and is governed by its maker's privacy terms, not ours; the app passes it the text to read and nothing else.

The Support page. Builds downloaded directly from https://sevenreadings.org/ or GitHub (not the app stores) show a "Support Seven Readings" page with a link to a payment page run by Stripe. Opening it takes you to Stripe in your browser; anything you enter there is between you and Stripe, under Stripe's privacy policy. The app does not know whether you opened it.

The website. https://sevenreadings.org/ is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's servers see the requests that fetch it, under GitHub's privacy statement. The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from any other domain; once opened it can run offline.

Children. The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes. This policy lives in the project's public repository (https://github.com/Joey-Fuentes/sevenreadings, store/privacy-policy.md); any change is visible in its history.
