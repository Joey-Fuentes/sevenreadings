# Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC), via the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible

The Westminster Leningrad Codex text is in the public domain
(`<rights>Public Domain</rights>` in the OSIS files).

The Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible markup we read it from is licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Required attribution, from
its LICENSE.md: "Original work of the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible
available at https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb."

Text shipped: `wlc/*.xml` from the repository pinned by commit in
`sources.toml`. Changes: morpheme separators and ketiv/qere notes are
dropped so that plain words remain; Masoretic verse numbers are mapped to
this app's canonical ids at ingest, with the Masoretic number kept as a
label.
