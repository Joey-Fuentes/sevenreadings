# World English Bible (WEB)

The World English Bible is in the public domain. ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm:
"The World English Bible is in the Public Domain. That means that it is
not copyrighted. However, 'World English Bible' is a Trademark of
eBible.org."

The trademark applies to the name, not the text: eBible.org asks that a
changed text not be called the World English Bible. We ship the text
unchanged (only footnotes, headings and cross-references are dropped), so
the name is used here to identify a faithful copy.

Text shipped: the USFM edition from eBible.org (`engwebp_usfm.zip`), pinned
by checksum in `sources.toml`.
