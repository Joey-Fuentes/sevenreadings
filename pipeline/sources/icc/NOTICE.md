# The International Critical Commentary

Volumes of the International Critical Commentary on the Holy Scriptures of
the Old and New Testaments (T&T Clark, Edinburgh; Charles Scribner's Sons,
New York) published before 1929: public domain in the United States by
date of publication, and in life-plus-seventy jurisdictions because their
authors died before 1956. Volumes published in 1929 or later are not used.

Text shipped: the Internet Archive's OCR (`_hocr.html`) of scans of
public-domain copies, pinned by checksum in `sources.toml`; the volumes
and their scans are listed there (`books`, `authors`, `urls`). The Archive
asserts no rights over these files; a faithful digitisation of a
public-domain book carries no new copyright (see `docs/licensing.md`).

The OCR is unproofread and it shows: this edition's ABBYY pass reads
"are" as "arc", loses the first word of some lines, and garbles Greek and
Hebrew. The text ships as the Archive made it, labelled as OCR; each note
is anchored from the page's running head ("[I. 1", "I. 1-7]") and the verse
number that opens it. Introductions, detached essays and indexes are not
included.
