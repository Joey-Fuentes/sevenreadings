# douay — Douay-Rheims Bible (Challoner revision)

The Douay-Rheims Bible (Rheims New Testament 1582, Douay Old Testament
1609-10) as revised by Bishop Richard Challoner (1749-52); public domain.

Text shipped: the "Bible Text" section of every chapter page of John Paul
Wohlscheid's Haydock transcription (<https://gitlab.com/JohnBlood/haydock>),
73 books. Cross-reference marks and bracketed references are removed; the
chapter summaries printed above each chapter are not kept. Numbering is the
Vulgate's and is renumbered to canonical ids at ingest (`native_ref` keeps
the Douay number), so Psalm 22 in this text is Psalm 23 in the others.

one pin.
